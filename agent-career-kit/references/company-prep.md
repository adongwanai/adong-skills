# Company And JD Preparation

Read this file for a representative JD, company pack, or application decision.

## Preserve Stable Resumes

A JD may change which existing evidence the candidate rehearses, not the primary resume facts or identity. Record repeated requirements across JDs in `jd-bank/market-signals.md`; use a single JD for one company pack.

## JD Decode

Separate:

1. `facts`: exact responsibilities, requirements, team/product statements, location, level, and process details stated by the JD or public source.
2. `must_have`: requirements expressed as necessary.
3. `nice_to_have`: optional or differentiating signals.
4. `hypotheses`: interpretations such as ownership, ambiguity, scale, research depth, or cross-team load. Quote the phrase that motivated each hypothesis and state how to verify it.
5. `candidate_evidence`: claim IDs that answer each requirement.
6. `gaps`: missing proof or preparation, not automatic resume edits.

Do not convert hiring language into unsupported company facts. Do not predict interview probability. Use `high`, `medium`, or `low` fit only with requirement-by-requirement evidence and unresolved gaps.

## Market Signal Bank

Across representative JDs, aggregate:

- recurring responsibilities and mechanisms;
- level indicators;
- company-specific themes;
- required production, training, evaluation, safety, or leadership evidence;
- topics that appear once and should not distort the long-term resume.

Update the capability map or learning/project priorities only when a pattern is meaningful.

## Company Pack

Create `outputs/interview/companies/<company>/prep.md` with:

1. role and source links with access dates;
2. factual product/team context;
3. requirement-to-claim mapping;
4. likely focus areas labeled as hypotheses;
5. selected 30-second and 2-minute stories;
6. technical, system design, algorithm, project, behavioral, and manager simulations;
7. candidate questions for the interviewer;
8. logistics and unresolved research.

Company-specific AgentGuide materials are simulations. Preserve that label and use their differentiated technical themes, not templated claims about real interview rounds.

## Supplied Simulation Theme Map

Use these only to vary practice. They are not current hiring facts, verified interview rounds, or evidence about the companies:

- `Alibaba/MOS-style`: vertical business constraints, context/RAG, browser or container tools, and end-to-end evaluation.
- `ByteDance/Seed-style`: coding Agent, runtime and sandbox design, plus experiment iteration speed.
- `Tencent/Hunyuan-style`: MCP and memory, multi-tenancy, task state, SLA and service governance.

When preparing a real company pack, verify current facts from the JD and public sources first. Keep the simulation theme label even when the name resembles a real company.
